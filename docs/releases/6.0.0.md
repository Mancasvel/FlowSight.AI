FlowSight 6.0 adds private online leagues with friends. Accept the service once, choose a unique username, and eligible focus points sync automatically while FlowSight runs, including in the background.

- Create a private group or join with an invitation to compare weekly points.
- Automatic sync starts when the app opens, updates after new observed activity, and retries when the connection returns. No manual sync button is required.
- Points belong to your signed-in account and unique username. Only newly observed focus after acceptance qualifies: 25 minutes earns 40 points, 50 earns 70, and 75 earns 100. Your five best days count each week.
- League sharing sends your username, league date and up to 75 eligible focus minutes with the identifiers needed to register the scoring device. Applications, titles, tasks and screenshots stay outside the league payload. League sharing is separate from history sync, cloud AI and product analytics.
- Disable leagues to stop sharing and request deletion of league data. Your local work history remains available. Offline deletion retries automatically.
- Navigation places Online first, Insights next, and the clock in the centre. The daily goal and its value share one row, with the progress bar restored below.

Windows: install the `-setup.exe` package, or use the MSI. Existing installations can update from the app. Your account, local history and saved preferences are retained.
