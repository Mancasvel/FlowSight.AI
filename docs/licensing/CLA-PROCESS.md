# CLA signature and merge process

Templates and badges do not prove signatures. No functioning automated CLA bot
is assumed. A maintainer must verify rights before merge.

1. Identify the contributor and employer/university rights; choose individual
   or corporate agreement and complete the actual Owner details.
2. Legally review and execute the exact version; preserve its SHA-256. Opening
   a PR does not sign it. Do not expose identity documents in a public PR.
3. Verify signature, identity, authority, covered commits/PRs and exclusions in
   a restricted registry outside this repo.
4. Review provenance and record the verification reference internally. Merge
   only covered contributions with sufficient rights.

The private registry records account identity, agreement hash/version, execution
date, authority, covered submissions and verification. Restrict access, define
retention and provide a privacy notice. Publish only justified minimal status.

Audit historic work separately; new templates do not retroactively license it
or amend old signatures. If a signature service is later configured, verify its
app permissions, agreement hash, storage/privacy and required merge check before
claiming automatic collection. Contact **manuel@flowsight.site** to arrange
execution. No contributors have been contacted or signatures collected in this
documentation update.
