Before tracking starts

FlowSight will observe the active application, selected interface elements, and periodic images of the active window while tracking is on.

  • Screenshots are analyzed by AI on this device and are never saved.
  • Only short activity summaries, categories, app names, and durations are kept locally.
  • Window titles are not stored by default. Local history is deleted after 30 days by default.
  • Common password managers are excluded by default; add other sensitive apps in Privacy & data.
  • Nothing is synced to FlowSight or an AI provider unless you separately enable it.
  • Opening at sign-in, automatic tracking, and focus reminders are separate choices in your profile.

Pause or stop tracking at any time. Do not use FlowSight to monitor another person without a valid lawful basis and the required workplace notice.

Local tracking

When you start tracking, the app processes images of the active window, active app and interface context on your device to create activity summaries. Images and raw interaction events stay in volatile memory and are discarded after local analysis. Excluded applications are skipped. This processing provides the feature you request. Summaries remain on your device for your selected retention period. If you enable focus reminders, local Qwen receives only verified aggregate counts of app switches or non-work browsing episodes. It can propose a reminder through an internal tool; FlowSight checks permission and cooldown before showing it. Reminders use generic wording by default. If you separately enable contextual reminders in Profile, the system notification may show a public app or site name and your selected task, but never a window title or URL. Those names stay on your device and are not sent to Qwen for reminder decisions.

Optional cloud purposes

  • Activity sync: sends locally generated aggregate summaries, categories, durations, ticket references and team/account IDs to FlowSight's Supabase service.
  • Cloud AI: sends your message, recent conversation and a seven-day local activity summary through FlowSight to Azure OpenAI for coaching. Cloud-generated individual reports use OpenRouter and its selected model provider.
  • Product analytics: sends a random installation ID, seven daily usage totals and the week's primary category. No account ID or email is included.
  • Integrations: Jira, Linear and Notion receive only data needed for actions you request under their own privacy terms.

Your choices and rights

Optional sharing is off by default and can be withdrawn here without affecting local tracking. You can export your data, erase local data, or delete your cloud account. You may also request access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or complain to your supervisory authority by contacting manuel@flowsight.site.

Retention and transfers

Local summaries use your selected period (30 days by default). Cloud activity and insight data is configured for 90 days, pseudonymous analytics for 35 days, and voluntary feedback for 12 months. Vendors may process data outside the EEA under an adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses. Account and statutory billing records may be retained only where legally required.

Before production, the controller must publish the completed Article 13 notice at a stable, accessible location. The current technical draft and implementation checklist are maintained in PRIVACY.md and docs/GDPR-COMPLIANCE.md.
